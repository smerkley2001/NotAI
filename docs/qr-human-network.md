# QR, sharing, gifts, checkout, and Human Network

## Implemented behavior

- Each physical unit receives an immutable random shirt token during server-side order creation. Its permanent address is `https://notaijusti.com/s/<token>`. Real QR SVG and 1024px PNG downloads use Q error correction and a four-module quiet zone. The sample checkerboard has been removed from the customizer. Codes activate only after a verified paid event; fully refunded orders disable their codes.
- Public scan pages reveal an optional story, optional design, and an opted-in handle. Personal names, emails, shipping data, account identifiers, and purchase details are never returned by the public functions. Shirt story and design visibility start off.
- Share links reveal a frozen personalization snapshot. Owners can revoke individual links. Recipients customize their own copy, preserving the original.
- Design starters, optional clearly labeled AI wording help, a common SVG proof renderer for the editor/proof/AI artwork/order pack, vector icons, size-guide content, catalog measurements, and reorder/another-size links are included. SVG proofs preserve wording and layout but fonts still need approval against the actual fulfillment template. No unverified measurements or model sizing are invented.
- Gift invitations require registered participants. The recipient submits their own design revision and active garment size. The giver reviews and copies that snapshot into a purchase; server checks prevent substituting a different gift. Giver purchase details remain in the giver's account; recipient controls the shirt story and QR identity. Invitation creation does not charge anyone. The giver supplies the recipient's delivery address in Stripe Checkout. Bearer invitation links can be claimed once; do not publish them indiscriminately. Gift purchases currently do not earn referral rewards.
- Checkout requires a registered, non-anonymous user and owned immutable revision. Database transactions derive product prices, snapshot personalization, create one shirt per unit, reserve credits, and validate gift/referral ownership. Browser price fields are never trusted. Checkout collects US shipping addresses, adds configured shipping, and enables Stripe automatic tax. It stays closed by default.
- Webhooks verify raw-body Stripe signatures and payment mode; database processing checks the bound session, currency, amounts, and delivery address. Unpaid completions do not confirm orders. Duplicate events cannot issue duplicate credit. $3 is earned per qualifying non-gift USD order, excluding self-referrals. Any cash refund reverses the referral reward; full refunds disable QR codes and return any store credit used. A refund after a reward was spent can leave a negative balance, recovered against future earnings. Refund amounts cannot move backwards when events arrive out of order.
- Available credits account for pending reservations. Credits discount the merchandise and leave at least $0.50 to charge. Expired/failed sessions release their reservation. Partial cash refunds do not automatically return used credits; full refunds do. Expired checkout requests require starting a new checkout, not reusing a closed Stripe session.
- Personal network displays qualifying connections up to three levels; private users are anonymous and traversal stops at them. Refunds hide the corresponding connection. Referral activity shows pending/qualified/rejected/reversed states.
- Merchant-only fulfillment desk downloads a confidential ZIP with order delivery data, frozen artwork, and one unique QR SVG per shirt. It records a real complete-order carrier handoff and exposes tracking in the customer's order page. It does not automatically send print jobs to a supplier. Only fully paid, unrefunded orders can be packed or shipped through this desk. Supplier submission, production dimensions, sample approval, partial shipments, and delivery-provider callbacks remain operational integrations.
