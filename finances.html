Dates use America/Chicago. Sales show paid orders created in this period, including their current refunds and lifetime order costs. Operating expenses use their recorded dates. This is an operational report, not a cash-flow or tax statement.

How the numbers work

Net revenue = shirt and shipping revenue − discounts, redeemed store credit and revenue refunds. Customer sales tax is excluded. Gross profit = net revenue − fulfillment costs. Contribution subtracts payment fees. Estimates are shown separately and never counted as recorded actual costs.

Profit totals stay incomplete until every order's costs have been reviewed. Partial-refund tax splits may be provisional. Outstanding store credit is shown separately; issuance is not deducted twice when credit is redeemed.

Printful costs and Stripe fees are entered manually for now. No automatic imports are connected.
