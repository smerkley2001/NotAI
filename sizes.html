Find your fit.

Measure a shirt you already love.

  1. Lay it flat without stretching it.
  2. Measure across the chest, just below the sleeves.
  3. Measure from the highest shoulder point to the bottom hem.
  4. Compare those measurements with the selected garment’s manufacturer size chart.

Shirt sizes vary by garment. The exact measurements, fabric, fit notes, and model sizes will be published once our fulfillment garment is selected.

If you prefer a relaxed fit, compare a relaxed shirt you own rather than guessing from a size letter.
